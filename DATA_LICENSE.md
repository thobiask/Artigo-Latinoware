# Data license and provenance

The PM2.5 snapshots under `data/pm25/` were derived from measurements made available by providers integrated into the OpenAQ platform. The applicable license may vary by original provider and must be checked in the API metadata.

- OpenAQ API v3 overview: https://docs.openaq.org/about/about
- OpenAQ licenses resource: https://docs.openaq.org/resources/licenses

The repository preserves the exact PM2.5 records used by the final computational proof of concept, plus inventories and hashes documenting the broader multi-pollutant study snapshot. Any redistribution or downstream use must respect source attribution and the terms of the original data provider.
